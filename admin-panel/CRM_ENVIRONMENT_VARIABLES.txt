Revision date: 2026-10-21
Scope: crm-audit-fixes-20261021 / PR #343
Do not paste real secret values into GitHub, chat, or browser-visible variables.

A. CLOUDFLARE PAGES FUNCTIONS — CRM PROJECT ONLY
Required server-side variables:
SUPABASE_URL=<Supabase project URL>
SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY=<Supabase service-role secret; server-side only>
ZOHO_CRM_WEBHOOK_SECRET=<random high-entropy secret, minimum 40 characters>

The Zoho inbound endpoint is:
POST /api/webhooks/zoho-mail
It requires Authorization: Bearer <ZOHO_CRM_WEBHOOK_SECRET>.
Configure a trusted Zoho Flow/custom function or server-side poller; adding this secret alone does not subscribe to Zoho Mail.

Do not enable either provider by enabling the other. Approval flags are not a substitute for testing sender identity, recipient consent, routing, and a controlled test plan.

E. IMPORTANT ROUTING AND SECURITY NOTES
- orders@fashionfussion.in is routed to the restricted admin_operations queue; do not reuse its credentials for general support replies.
- SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY must never be prefixed with PUBLIC, VITE_, NEXT_PUBLIC_, or placed in browser code.
- Do not use SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY for server-side database operations.
- The CRM Zoho webhook and the unified HMAC webhook are separate endpoints with different secrets; do not interchange ZOHO_CRM_WEBHOOK_SECRET and CRM_EMAIL_WEBHOOK_SECRET.
- This checklist documents required names only. It does not confirm that any secret currently exists, is valid, or has been configured in Cloudflare/Supabase.
