# UPI merchant MDR accounting

Effective 15 October 2026 IST, eligible UPI merchant payments above ₹2,000 use a 0.4% fallback MDR estimate, capped at ₹300 per transaction.

This is a merchant-side payment-processing cost. It must never be added as a customer checkout surcharge or folded into marketplace commission.

Actual provider processing fees are the source of truth and take precedence over the estimate. Explicitly exempt/ineligible transactions resolve to zero estimated MDR. LaunchPad 0% marketplace commission remains independent of payment-processing costs.
