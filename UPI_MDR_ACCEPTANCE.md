# Acceptance criteria

- ₹2,000 eligible UPI: ₹0 estimated MDR.
- ₹3,000 eligible UPI: ₹12.
- ₹5,000 eligible UPI: ₹20.
- ₹75,000+ eligible UPI: maximum ₹300.
- Pre-effective-date, non-UPI or exempt: ₹0 estimate.
- Positive actual provider fee: actual fee only; no additional estimate.
- Customer checkout payable remains unchanged.
