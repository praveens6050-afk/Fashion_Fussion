Security

Account and payment safety

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Fashion_Fussion uses account access controls, secure checkout safeguards and payment confirmation checks to protect customer shopping and order activity. Final payable values are shown before order placement.

Payment safety: use only the payment options shown during Fashion_Fussion checkout. If a payment status is unclear, check My Orders before paying again and contact Customer Care if you need help.

Checkout and payments

  • Review the final product value, GST, discounts, delivery charge and total payable before placing an order.
  • Online payment credentials should be entered only in the secure payment interface shown during checkout.
  • Cash on Delivery may be offered when it is available for the order.
  • If a payment appears successful but the order status is unclear, do not immediately pay again. Check My Orders or contact Customer Care.

Account safety

  • Use a strong, unique password for your Fashion_Fussion account.
  • Keep your email and phone details accurate so account and order communication reaches you.
  • Do not share your password or account access with another person.
  • Only your authorized account should be used to view your saved details and order history.

Payment secrets

Fashion_Fussion Customer Care will never ask you to share your OTP, card CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password. Do not send these details in a support ticket, email, phone call or chat.
