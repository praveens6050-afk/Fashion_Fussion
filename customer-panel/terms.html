Catalogue, pricing, GST and promotions

  • Catalogue information reflects products and variants currently offered by the store.

Payments and order creation

Online payment and Cash on Delivery options may be offered at checkout depending on the order. Fashion_Fussion currently adds no separate prepaid or COD payment-handling fee.

  • Use the payment option shown during checkout and review the final payable amount before confirming.
  • If payment status is unclear, check My Orders before trying again or contact Customer Care.
  • Use My Orders or Order Details for order-specific confirmation and progress.
