Privacy & your rights

Data deletion instructions

You may request deletion of your personal information associated with Fashion Fussion. These instructions also apply to information we process through Meta services, including WhatsApp interactions.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Email customer.care@fashionfussion.in with the subject Fashion Fussion - Data Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us whether you want to delete your customer or seller account and associated personal information, specific personal information, or your WhatsApp/Meta-related interaction data.
  3. To help us locate the data, provide the email address associated with your Fashion Fussion account, or the mobile number used to contact us on WhatsApp. You may contact us even if you never created a Fashion Fussion account.
  4. We will review the request, take reasonable steps to verify that you are authorized to make it, and explain the next steps. We will confirm once the request has been handled, subject to any legally required retention.

What happens after you contact us?

We may ask for additional information to verify account ownership or the phone number connected with a WhatsApp conversation before acting. We review relevant records and arrange deletion or de-identification of eligible data in our systems and, where applicable, with service providers. We respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Some records, such as invoices, tax documentation, payment records, fraud-prevention information, or dispute records, may need to be retained when required or permitted by law. We will restrict retained information to the relevant purposes.

For your security: never email a password, one-time password (OTP), card details, UPI PIN, or other login secrets. Sending a request does not instantly or automatically delete an account. Deletion is reviewed and handled through our support process.

Other questions

If you cannot access your registered email or you only interacted with us through WhatsApp, email our support team from an email address you can access and explain the situation. We will guide you through appropriate verification.
