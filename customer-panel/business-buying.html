Two larger-quantity paths

The product page decides which options are available from the current catalogue configuration.

  • Configured bulk pricing: on bulk-enabled products, choose Bulk / Business, enter a supported quantity and review any live quantity tier shown on that product.
  • Business quote: for larger configured quantities, the product page can expose Request Business Quote. The current storefront shows that option at 100+ pieces, or at the product minimum quantity when that minimum is higher.

Pricing, quantities and stock

  • Retail price remains the normal product price unless a configured bulk tier or accepted business quote applies.
  • Bulk tier prices shown on a product come from the live catalogue and are recalculated or verified by the backend at checkout.
  • The current product quantity controls cap a single product quantity at 500 pieces.
  • No automatic discount is promised merely because a customer selects a larger quantity.
  • The storefront currently does not display authoritative stock availability because the active product catalogue does not expose an inventory field to customers.

Business quotes

  • A business profile must be saved before a quote request is submitted.
  • A quote request uses the selected product, requested quantity and optional customer note.
  • Submitting a request does not reserve inventory.
  • Quoted pricing is not the same as an order. A valid quote must be accepted before quote checkout can use the negotiated amount.
  • An accepted quote that has already been used for an order cannot be checked out again.

GST and invoices

Fashion_Fussion is GST and Udyam/MSME registered. Customers may save an optional GSTIN and billing note in the business profile. GST invoice eligibility depends on the order and applicable tax requirements; this page does not promise invoice eligibility for every transaction.

Payments, delivery and returns

Business and larger-quantity orders remain subject to the applicable store checkout and order policies. Review the Store Terms, Shipping & Delivery and Returns & Refunds pages for the current rules.
