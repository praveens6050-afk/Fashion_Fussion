Pricing, quantities and availability

  • Retail price remains the normal product price unless an eligible bulk price or accepted business quote applies.
  • Bulk prices shown on a product apply when available and the final payable amount is confirmed at checkout.
  • Current quantity controls may limit how many pieces can be entered in one order request.
  • No automatic discount is promised merely because a larger quantity is selected.
  • Availability can change and is not guaranteed until the order is confirmed for fulfilment.

Business quotes

  • Save your business profile before submitting a quote request.
  • A quote request includes the selected product, requested quantity and any optional note you provide.
  • Submitting a request does not reserve inventory.
  • A quote must be accepted before it can be used at quote checkout.
  • A quote that has already been used for an order cannot be used again.
