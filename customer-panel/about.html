How shopping works

  1. Retail shopping: customers can browse products and buy one or a few pieces through the normal cart and checkout flow.
  2. Bulk pricing: eligible products may offer quantity-based pricing.
  3. Business quotes: eligible larger quantities may offer a reviewed quote-request path. A request does not reserve inventory or guarantee a special price.
  4. Account choice: customers can choose a Personal Customer or Business / Bulk Buyer profile depending on their needs.
Show clear prices
