How shopping works

  • Retail shopping: customers can browse active catalogue products and buy one or a few pieces through the normal cart and checkout flow.
  • Configured bulk pricing: a product may expose Bulk / Business mode only when larger-quantity buying is configured for that product.
  • Business quotes: eligible larger quantities may offer a reviewed quote-request path. A request does not reserve inventory or guarantee a special price.
  • One account: the same customer account is used for orders, saved business details and business quote activity.
Show real configured values

Bulk discounts, quote values and order totals should come from the actual store configuration or verified backend result rather than marketing assumptions.

Avoid stock promises

The current storefront does not present authoritative stock availability when the active customer catalogue has no inventory field to display.

Keep support discoverable

Customers can reach order support, public policies, business buying information, email support and phone support from the store.

Business buying

Business customers can use configured product quantity tiers or the reviewed quote workflow where available. Quote acceptance, discounts, stock, GST invoice eligibility, response times and delivery dates are not guaranteed merely because a business customer requests a larger quantity.
