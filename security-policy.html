Security & Privacy

How we protect your checkout and data

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Fashion_Fussion operates an authenticated ecommerce checkout. Product totals, GST, promotions and delivery charges are calculated or verified by the server before an order is finalized.

Payment model: prepaid payments use Razorpay-hosted checkout and are verified server-side. Cash on Delivery may also be available when the checkout offers it. Fashion_Fussion does not add a separate prepaid or COD payment-handling fee. The checkout displays the authoritative payable amount before the order is placed.

We do not ask you to enter card numbers, CVV values, UPI PINs or net-banking passwords into Fashion_Fussion pages. Prepaid payment credentials are handled by Razorpay's payment interface.

Payment security and recovery

  • Razorpay orders are created by the backend using server-side credentials.
  • The browser cannot set the authoritative product price, GST rate, discount or final payable amount.
  • Prepaid responses are verified on the server and captured payments are cross-checked against the stored order, user and amount.
  • Checkout finalization includes state and duplicate-processing protections.
  • If a browser callback is interrupted, server-side reconciliation support is available. Customers should check My Orders before attempting a second payment.
  • Cash on Delivery orders do not pass through Razorpay.

Account and database security

  • Checkout requires an authenticated Supabase account.
  • Row Level Security is used on customer-facing database tables to restrict records by the signed-in user and administrator role.
  • Administrator-only product cost data is not exposed through the public catalogue.
  • Cart data may be stored in the browser, but final checkout values are recalculated or verified by the backend.

Third-party services

Fashion_Fussion uses Supabase for authentication and database services, Razorpay for prepaid payment processing, and Vercel for production hosting and backend APIs.

Data retention

Account, address, order, payment-reference and support records may be retained as reasonably required to operate the store, provide support, prevent fraud and meet accounting or legal obligations.
