Overview

Fashion_Fussion ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to sell home, kitchen, beauty, and lifestyle products. This Security Policy explains how we handle the security of your data when you browse, place an order, or contact us — and what we do (and don't yet do) around payments and site security today.

Where we are right now: Fashion_Fussion is an early-stage storefront. Checkout uses Razorpay-hosted payment UI. The site never receives full card/UPI credentials. A server-side order is created and the payment response is verified before an order is treated as paid. We're documenting our practices honestly rather than claiming certifications we don't yet hold.

Data we collect

When you use this site or place an order, we may collect:

InformationWhy we collect it
Name, phone number, emailTo confirm and deliver your order, and respond to enquiries
Delivery addressTo ship your order
Order contents & cart activityTo process orders and improve our catalog
Messages you send us (email/phone)To handle support, returns, and bulk order requests

We do not ask for or store card numbers, UPI PINs, CVV, or net-banking passwords on this site. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

Payment security

  • Payment processing is handled by Razorpay — we will never see or store your full card number.
  • Payments, once enabled, will be transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
  • Cash-on-delivery, if offered, is settled directly with our delivery partner and does not involve any card data.
  • If a payment attempt fails or looks suspicious, we may contact you by phone or email to verify the order before dispatch.

Account & browsing security

  • This site is served over HTTPS wherever deployed, encrypting data between your browser and our server.
  • We do not currently require account creation to browse or order — cart contents are held only in your browser's active session and are not stored on our servers.
  • We recommend keeping your own device and browser updated, and avoiding shared/public devices when sharing personal delivery details.

Third parties

We may share the minimum necessary order information (name, phone, address) with:

  • Delivery/courier partners, to fulfil your order
  • Our payment gateway provider, for payment processing
  • Our wholesale supplier, only where needed to fulfil a specific product order

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention

We keep order and contact records for as long as needed to handle deliveries, returns, warranty or legal obligations, and basic bookkeeping — typically no longer than necessary for these purposes. You can ask us to delete your personal data at any time (see below), subject to any records we're legally required to keep.
