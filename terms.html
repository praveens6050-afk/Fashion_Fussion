These terms summarize how the current Fashion_Fussion storefront operates for retail customers and larger-quantity or business buyers. Order-specific prices, availability and status shown by the store remain the source of truth for an individual order.

Accounts and customer information

  • Checkout requires a signed-in Fashion_Fussion account under the current storefront workflow.
  • Customers should keep account access secure and provide usable contact and delivery information for order fulfilment and support.
  • Saved addresses should be reviewed before order placement because the selected address becomes part of the order record.

Catalogue, pricing, GST and promotions

  • Catalogue information reflects the products and variants currently made available through the store.
  • Availability, quantity limits and product details can change as catalogue or inventory data changes.
  • The final product value, GST, discount, delivery charge and payable total are shown or verified through the checkout workflow.
  • A coupon, gift card, business price or other discount applies only when the current store rules accept it; no bulk discount is implied simply because a larger quantity is selected.

Payments and order creation

Prepaid checkout currently uses Razorpay-hosted payment processing. Cash on Delivery may also be offered where the checkout presents that option. Fashion_Fussion currently adds no separate prepaid or COD payment-handling fee.

  • Prepaid payment responses are verified by the backend before the stored order is treated as paid.
  • If a payment callback is interrupted, customers should check My Orders before attempting another payment and use support when the status is unclear.
  • An order record and its current status in My Orders or Order Details should be used for order-specific confirmation and progress.

Shipping and delivery

Delivery charges, saved-address handling, fulfilment stages and the current position on delivery timing are documented in the Shipping & Delivery policy. Fashion_Fussion does not publish an unsupported fixed delivery-day, courier or nationwide-service guarantee.

Cancellations, returns, exchanges and refunds

Eligibility depends on the order state and the current return/refund workflow. The dedicated Returns, Cancellations & Refunds policy explains the available customer actions, delivery-charge treatment and support-assisted cases.

Business and larger-quantity buying

Fashion_Fussion supports larger-quantity buying within the same store and can expose business quote workflows where available. A requested quantity or submitted quote request does not itself guarantee stock, a special price or acceptance. Any business price or payable amount should be taken from the accepted/current store workflow.

Privacy and security

Customer-data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. Account, checkout and payment-protection information is described separately on the Security page.
